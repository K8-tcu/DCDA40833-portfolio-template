Visualization

Critique

Information Resolution

The infographic translates the data into a more visually understandable format using simple, bold tactics such as color and mini graphs. The data in the infographic shows that California Teens are being arrested less for a wide array of crimes. This specifically includes violence, burglary, drugs, sex crimes, curfew/truancy, and others. The translation of this data into a visual is easy to understand. The graphic is compiled of mini graphs with bold colors and downward-sloping lines. Using these downward-trending lines sends a clear message of a decrease in the given topics. Each graph also has the number of arrests, that show the overall trend to provide more information to the reader. With these positives, one thing I would fix in this infographic for the reader is to present the same information in each graph. The way it is currently set up only adds the graph labels to one of the six graphs. This could be confusing to some readers in terms of how the data is presented. Overall, I think the graph does a good job of conveying the information with eye-catching colors and downward-trending lines. The work balances integrity and aesthetic by making it simple and including trend percentages with clear subtitles.

Metaphor & Meaning

Metaphor & Meaning uses metaphors appropriate to the data to clarify the information for the reader. In this infographic, the mini graphs are arranged in two rows and convey meaning through their downward slopes. This is a universally understood way to show something going down. This makes sense, as the title is “California’s Teens Are Behaving Better.” This implies arrests are going down, given the subtitle right under the main title that says “Arrests for under 18s”. When analyzing this infographic, one thing I was stuck on was the title. Just because arrests are down doesn’t necessarily mean the teens are behaving better.

Emotional Manipulation vs. Engagement

Teens could still be doing these things without getting arrested. The concept of “good behavior” is also relatively subjective. One’s idea of good behavior could very reasonably require more moral actions than solely not getting arrested. This means that assuming teens in California are behaving better may be an overly vague explanation, with emotional manipulation intended to get readers' attention. All of the data is correct, but this title may lead to misunderstanding.

Accessability

In terms of accessibility, the graph is easy to understand for the average person, but some small details may be hard to follow. The text hierarchy in the image is clear, and the bold lettering makes a dark contrast. When looking at the code behind the image, this graphic becomes less accessible without alt text for those using a text–to–speech reader.

Conclusion

Overall, this infographic does a good job of representing the data. There are small things that need to change but it does an adequate job how it is.
