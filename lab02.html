AI Tool Evaluation

Introduction

Here, in Lab 2, we analyzed different AI tools after giving them prompts relevant to our future career path. Then, we were able to think about the future and implications of AI.

Tool 1: Claude

Capabilities

The tool did well at generating specific copy for marketing and communication. I gave it a prompt similar to one I was given in one of my classes. It was good in tone, content, and format. It might be worth asking more follow-up questions, since the copy was slightly vague. For example, it made up a mission statement for an agency because it wasn’t provided one: “We exist to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world impact. We're here to help your brand tell its story, connect with its audience, and achieve measurable success—all while developing the next generation of advertising and PR leaders.”

Appropriate Use

This tool is good for career use. When setting up my account, it asked what industry I would use it for, suggesting a professional perspective. It also knew about certain formats for specialized marketing documents.

Ethical Considerations

I worry about the ethical implications of people's reliance on AI. It can clearly do a pretty good job of communication, so people will use it to do their jobs. Over time, I see this taking jobs in the industry. As I’m entering the communication/marketing industry, I wonder whether I will need to change career paths as I grow older and AI improves.

Tool 2: Leonardo.ai

Capabilities

The tool did well in creating photo and video content from scratch. It provided sample prompts and the results produced by those prompts. This helped me figure out what I would ask for. One thing that could be better is that the tool ignored some of the details I put in the prompt. It produced photos and videos in the right direction, but many smaller details were missing.

Appropriate Use

This tool is good for exploration, where it isn’t crucial that everything comes out exactly correct. It produced fun, creative outputs, but it wasn’t what I was expecting from my prompt.

Ethical Considerations

I worry that this photo and video tool will become so good that it’s hard to tell what is human-made and what is AI-generated. I already see this with some AI political videos. People are faking incidents and making people say things they didn't actually say. This is really unethical, as fake news has been a big problem in our country.

Broader Reflections

Personal Use

I use AI tools to help me make corrections in my design and Photoshop projects. In my free time, I am a photographer, so I use AI integrations with certain apps to streamline my editing process. At work, I am encouraged to use AI for ideation. It has helped me a lot with brainstorming, getting ideas flowing for projects and events.

DCDA Context

DCDA has helped me better understand AI and its cultural context. I think AI will hurt the coding aspect of this program, as it has gotten much better at coding over the years. I also think AI will help DCDA, as the program is very forward-thinking about it and has gotten ahead of understanding and utilizing it. I could see DCDA adapting to have more AI-centered classes. Despite AI’s growing influence, digital culture is still heavily reliant on human input. People define culture, and there’s so much more to it than AI outputs.

Evolving Landscape

I wonder if AI will ever have a disclaimer. I think it could be helpful in addressing the growth of fake news and related claims.
