Reflection & Analysis

Design Inspiration

My basemap design was based off of a painting of California hills (see above). The sunset on the hills caused a pink, orange, and light green color palette. I used these colors in my map to give off the same vibe. Major streets are pink, the water is a light blue, the land is shades of light green, and the roads are a light orangey brown. This creates a realistic vibe that still corresponds to my painting.

Cartographic Challenges

One of the main cartographic challenges I faced was choosing a color for each element that would both match my painting I was inspired by and make sense to viewers. I needed people to look at my map and understand what they were looking at when scrolling on different zoom levels. I also need them to have good contrast so it was clear where the lines/map pins were. I also had to consider the layers and how the colors would look to add depth without covering up detail.

Technical Challenges

There weren't many coding challenges I didn't run into in this lab. I really struggled with putting together code from scratch to create my custom map with specific touristy locations. I used AI to help me fix broken code I had written. That helped, but AI is not perfect and can often make mistakes or produce code that doesn't work as expected. I needed to fall back onto my knowledge of Python from previous classes to produce a working result.
